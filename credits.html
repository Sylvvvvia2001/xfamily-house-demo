House playback

Credits

House v102 · Interactive household activity playback. The four dates shown belong to S01.

Rendered with three.js (MIT license). Quick-load geometry uses meshoptimizer (MIT license). Scene materials include textures from Poly Haven. Character movement incorporates project capture, Adobe Mixamo, the Carnegie Mellon University Motion Capture Database, and CIRCLE. Object interaction includes OakInk. House furnishings include assets sourced from BlendSwap, Sketchfab, and CGTrader.
